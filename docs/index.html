The spread, in plain numbers. On 7 Nov 2026 the mid-market rate was ₹94.14 per dollar — but Indian banks' TT buying rates trailed it: Central Bank of India ₹93.50, City Union Bank ₹93.55, DBS ₹92.28. That gap of ₹0.80–₹3.77 per dollar is the spread: on $500 it quietly costs you ₹400–₹1,885. The rate on this page is the ECB reference (mid-market) rate — the honest benchmark, not your bank's rate.
Bank TT rates as reported 7 Nov 2026; mid-market ₹94.14. AED is not published by the ECB, so it is not listed here.
